\paragraph{Slot / program decomposition for RAG.} Slot-based and
program-guided RAG frameworks decompose a multi-hop question into typed
retrieval steps or executable programs, promising compositional evidence.
Our slot compiler shares that goal but is evaluated under a strict matched
budget with adapted baselines, which most such systems do not report.

\paragraph{Retrieval-augmented multi-hop reasoning.} Iterative-retrieval
agents (IRCoT) and graph-retrieval systems (GraphRAG) are among the
strongest adapted baselines on these benchmarks and are our comparison
points. Our finding that a slot system can win one dataset, tie another, and
lose two on inherent selection accuracy is scoped relative to these adapted
baselines under the shared protocol.

\paragraph{Honest / matched-budget evaluation.} There is a growing call to
report RAG results under identical budgets and to separate quality from
budget accounting. Our separate reporting of \texttt{acc\_full} and
\texttt{acc\_ok}, our disclosure of per-run non-determinism, and our
full-negative-result reporting for rejected interventions are in that spirit:
we treat what did not work as first-class evidence.
