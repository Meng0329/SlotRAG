\subsection{Budget-Fix Ablation: From False Collapse to Real Accuracy}\label{sec:results:budget}

A raw $\S$4.3 accounting penalizes the guard on the cost of \emph{dual-access
bundles}: a slot opening two binding contexts issues one 2-query batch, which
counts as two physical calls against $\max\_\mathrm{retrieval\_calls}=4$.
Cross-slot accumulation drives $49.4\%$ (MuSiQue) and $32.6\%$ (HotpotQA) of
items to \texttt{BE}, scored as 0 --- a \emph{structural accounting collapse},
not a quality signal.

Two interventions fix it. H-029 (bound-single degradation) drops a bound
slot's 2-query bundle to one anchored question+lexical query. H-030 (cross-slot
budget reservation) front-reserves future slots and prunes plans that cannot
fit. Together (guard-budget), budget-exceeded collapses to $0$ on MuSiQue
($428\!\rightarrow\!0$) and HotpotQA ($326\!\rightarrow\!0$), 2WikiMultiHop
$75\!\rightarrow\!37$, DROP stays $0$. Recovered items are high-completion
(MuSiQue mean 0.547, HotpotQA 0.764); matched both-ok pairs show no systematic
regression ($143$w/$124$l/$2771$t). The headline gains:

\medskip
\centerline{
  MuSiQue $0.3171\rightarrow0.5807$ (WIN), \quad
  HotpotQA $0.5312\rightarrow0.7842$ (TIE) }
\medskip
The two remaining sets are \emph{inherent} gaps, not budget: DROP ran 0
\texttt{BE} throughout, and 2WikiMultiHop still trails IRCoT by 0.055 after
recovering all recoverable items. Budget-fix alone does not raise Coverage; it
surfaces the true 25\%.

\subsection{Failure Attribution: Selection Ceilings}\label{sec:results:failure}

Across 2000 items (HotpotQA + 2wiki, guard-budget), failures split into
\emph{surface-form} truncation ($\sim$88 HotpotQA / $\sim$3 2wiki; predicted
answer is a substring of gold, e.g.\ \emph{Duane Clarridge} vs
\emph{Duane ``Dewy'' Clarridge}), \emph{alias-in-evidence} selection failure
($159$ / $214$; gold verbatim in the evidence yet the model picks another
entity), and \emph{alias-not-in-evidence} retrieval gaps ($56$ / $97$).

Four string-correction heuristics (longest-substring completion, boundary
truncation, parenthetical completion, and a distribution proof of
infeasibility) are \emph{all net-negative}, e.g.\ longest-substring completion
nets $-452$ (HotpotQA) / $-346$ (2wiki) by pulling answers into full
sentences. Because ``pred is a substring of gold'' is rare ($<5\%$) while
``pred is contained in longer evidence'' is common ($>40\%$), no
gold-free deterministic rule can extend short answers without breaking many
correct ones. This is the selection-ceiling evidence (H-022, confirmed $3\times$):
gold answers are verbatim in the evidence and post-processing yields no gain.
The correct intervention would be a stronger selector, which is out of scope
under the frozen-model condition.

StrategyQA is a robust TIE, not a WIN: three-run mean guard $0.8815$ vs IRCoT
$0.8833$ ($\Delta=-0.19$pt), with the decoder flip-flopping on arithmetic
boundary items at temperature 0.
