\paragraph{Matched budget ($\S$4.3).}  All systems---\textsc{SlotRAG-X} and every
baseline---share one protocol: model Qwen3.6-27B \cite{qwen3} (internal endpoint),
$\max\_\mathrm{tokens}=2048$, temperature $0.0$, $\max\_\mathrm{steps}=4$,
$\max\_\mathrm{llm\_calls}=64$, $\max\_\mathrm{retrieval\_calls}=4$, and
$300$~s/question timeout. Budget-exceeded items score $0.0$ for every method,
so coverage comparisons are not inflated by one side ignoring the budget.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}  Five multi-hop benchmarks: HotpotQA \cite{hotpotqa}, 2WikiMultiHop \cite{2wikimultihop},
MuSiQue \cite{musique}, StrategyQA \cite{strategyqa}, and DROP \cite{drop}. Samples are drawn from the SEALED final
test sets, isolated from development, with exposed samples excluded.
Headlined cells use $n=867/1000/1000/1000$ (musique/hotpotqa/2wiki/drop);
StrategyQA is evaluated on its full clean set ($n=180$, averaged over three
runs, $\S$\ref{sec:results:failure}). Paired sampling: baseline and
\textsc{SlotRAG-X} share identical sample IDs; sample size per dataset is
power-sufficient for a $5\%$ effect ($\alpha=0.05$, power $0.80$; required
$n\in[8,18]$, all headlined $n\gg$ that).

\paragraph{Baselines.}  Strongest per-dataset adapted baselines from the
coverage ledger, run under the same matched budget and adapted (not exact
upstream) implementations: MuSiQue $\rightarrow$ IRCoT \cite{ircot} ($0.5263$), HotpotQA
$\rightarrow$ GraphRAG ($0.8124$), 2WikiMultiHop $\rightarrow$ IRCoT
($0.7449$), DROP $\rightarrow$ GraphRAG \cite{graphrag} ($0.7246$). We state plainly that all
baselines are \emph{adapted}; we never claim to beat exact upstream SOTA.

\paragraph{WIN/TIE/LOSS.}  A cell is a WIN when $|\Delta|>0.03$ against its
strongest adapted baseline, a TIE when $|\Delta|\le 0.03$, and a LOSS
otherwise; Coverage is the fraction of datasets won. Non-determinism of the
decoder on boundary questions (flip rates $0$--$3.9\%$) is disclosed and
SEALED results are averaged over runs with run-to-run ranges reported.

\paragraph{Metric and baseline disclosures.}  \texttt{acc\_full}/primary
score is Exact-Match on HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHop, MuSiQue and drop\_F1 on
DROP; collapsing these into a single Coverage percentage mixes per-domain
metrics, and we report the Coverage figure with that caveat explicit. All
baselines are \emph{adapted} (not exact upstream) and run under the matched
budget; the strongest baseline identity/values changed across phases on two
datasets (2WikiMultiHop strongest moved react~$\rightarrow$~IRCoT,
$0.7936\rightarrow0.7449$; DROP GraphRAG $0.7613\rightarrow0.7246$), so
``strongest adapted baseline'' is defined per the matched protocol at the
time of this evaluation and should not be read as a stable absolute SOTA.
The headlined four cells (n=867/1000/1000/1000) are single-run; StrategyQA
alone is 3-run averaged, and the hotpotqa TIE margin (0.0282) is within the
decoder's disclosed flip range.
