# REVIEWER_SELECTION_GUIDE.md — Suggested Reviewer Criteria

> **Date**: 2026-09-08
> **Note**: No specific reviewer names or emails are pre-filled. Authors must
> select reviewers manually based on the criteria below and their knowledge of
> the field.

---

## Criteria

Candidate reviewers should have recent, verifiable expertise in **at least one** of:

1. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems
2. Query optimization / query processing (database or IR)
3. Knowledge/data engineering (knowledge graphs, evidence management)
4. Multi-hop question answering / multi-hop retrieval
5. Budget- or cost-aware information retrieval

## Exclusion criteria (mandatory)

- No recent coauthor relationship (within ~5 years)
- No same institution as any author
- No obvious advisor/advisor-student relationship with any author
- No known reviewer conflict (funding, collaboration, personal)

## Suggested reviewer pool shape

| Expertise | Priority |
|-----------|----------|
| RAG systems (IRCoT, ReAct, self-RAG line) | High |
| Query optimization (PlanRAG, PyRAG line) | High |
| Knowledge/data engineering (graph retrieval) | Medium |
| Multi-hop QA benchmarks | Medium |

## Verification steps

1. Confirm at least one of the priority expertise areas from recent
   publications (last 5 years)
2. Run conflict-of-interest check against all authors
3. Confirm reviewer is not at the same institution
4. If the portal asks, provide 3-5 reviewers (TKDE typically allows
   suggestions but the editor-in-chief makes the final choice)
