Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are submitting our manuscript, "SlotRAG: Budget-Feasible Physical Planning over Typed Evidence Plans for Multi-Hop Retrieval-Augmented Generation," for consideration as a regular paper in IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE).

Multi-hop retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems decompose complex questions into multiple evidence-acquisition steps. Under a hard global retrieval-call budget, a decomposition that is logically valid need not be physically executable: independent per-slot allocations can sum to exceed the budget, causing budget exhaustion before answer generation. This is an execution-layer problem in data engineering: the gap between a compiled evidence plan and its feasible materialization under resource constraints.

This manuscript makes three contributions. First, it introduces typed evidence plans, a separation of logical evidence requirements (slots, joins, operators) from their physical materialization strategy, exposing structural properties for compile-time analysis. Second, it derives a compile-time budget-feasibility condition: a necessary condition for static budget exhaustion, confirmed against frozen matched-budget executions on 350 deep plans with perfect recall and 0.533 precision. Third, it develops a structure-sensitive dispatch rule that applies a budget-aware physical planner selectively, improving answer quality by 7.7 EM points on deep plans while protecting shallow plans from harm.

We believe this work fits TKDE's scope in knowledge and data engineering: it addresses query execution and physical planning for a class of knowledge retrieval workloads, evaluates system behavior under a matched performance budget, and reports the statistical evidence honestly, including the limitation that independent shallow-regime confirmation was infeasible under the frozen protocol.

The manuscript is original, is not under review elsewhere, and all authors have approved the submission. We have prepared source files, figures, a supplementary document, and a reproduction guide to support review.

Thank you for considering this submission.
