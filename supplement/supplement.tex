\section{Pre-registration History (H-STRUCT)} \label{sec:hstruct}

The evaluation follows a frozen pre-registered protocol. The hypothesis chain
is archived as: \texttt{H-STRUCT-1} (budget-feasibility diagnosis; census of
deep eligible plans), \texttt{H-STRUCT-2} (paired matched-budget confirmation
of the flat planner on deep plans), \texttt{H-STRUCT-3} (structure-gate
dispatch necessity, exploratory replay), and \texttt{H-STRUCT-4}
(independent shallow-regime confirmation). Pre-registration documents,
amendments, and final reports are versioned in the repository under

\section{Statistical Protocol} \label{sec:stats}

All paired comparisons share the same compiled plans, questions, and seeds.
The primary test is the two-sided exact McNemar test (binomial exact test on
discordant pairs). Confidence intervals are paired bootstrap percentiles
(10{,}000 iterations, seed 2027, full-$N$ question-level resampling). Dataset
heterogeneity is assessed with Holm-corrected tests across datasets and
strata. Budget-exceeded items score 0.0 EM because evaluation uses
exact-match scoring and budget-exhausted executions without a final answer
receive EM~$=0$. The confirmatory budget is $B=8$
($\max\_\mathrm{steps}=8$, $\max\_\mathrm{retrieval\_calls}=8$,
$\max\_\mathrm{llm\_calls}=96$); an earlier permissive exploratory trace used
higher limits and is explicitly labeled \emph{exploratory}.

\section{H-STRUCT-4: Infeasible Confirmation Attempt} \label{sec:hstruct4}

A confirmatory shallow-regime matched-budget evaluation was pre-registered
with n~=~1,428 required (power analysis, $\alpha=0.05$, 80\% power). The V1.2
frozen census did not persist full SlotPlan JSON for the 6,133 shallow
questions---only structural metadata and plan hashes---and the protocol
prohibited re-compilation. Zero answers were generated; the attempt produced
no counterexamples. The gate's shallow-regime benefit therefore remains
supported only by exploratory evidence; an independent confirmation under the
frozen matched-budget protocol remains an open requirement.

\section{Additional Failure Analysis} \label{sec:failure}

Static budget-exhausted items had average EM below 0.01, confirming that
partial evidence rarely yields correct answers on deep multi-hop questions.
Budget exhaustion is structural: it arises from the mismatch between local
slot allocations and the global budget (41.7\% of deep static executions),
not from retrieval failures or generation errors. The flat planner eliminates
these events by construction ($\sum_s a_s \leq B$).
